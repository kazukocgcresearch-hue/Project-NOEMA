NOEMA-OrganFarm extends the Project NOEMA research line from serialized epistemic procedures toward conditional latent cognitive execution.

The architecture is built on a largely frozen Qwen3-14B-Base and introduces separately trainable latent “Organs” with restricted functional responsibilities, connected through Functional Freeze, latent-state inheritance, deterministic routing, and state-dependent conditional execution.
