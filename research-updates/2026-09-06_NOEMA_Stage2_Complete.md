## Project NOEMA — Stage 2 Complete

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** `STAGE 2 COMPLETE`

Project NOEMA has reached its Stage 2 milestone.

During this phase, the training strategy was redesigned from direct natural-language reasoning toward a constrained intermediate representation optimized for stable cognitive operations.

The current model can:

* preserve unresolved conditions instead of prematurely resolving them;
* identify which conditions remain unresolved;
* infer what additional evidence would be required to resolve those conditions;
* retain the resulting judgment structure across unseen evaluation cases.

This marks the completion of the current reverse-reasoning stage.

### Next

Stage 3 will focus on converting the internal judgment state into natural-language responses without altering the underlying reasoning result.

> Implementation details of the internal representation and routing mechanism are currently retained as private Project NOEMA Core components.
